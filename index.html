Before you begin

  • Use a desktop/laptop with headphones or speakers.
  • Allow microphone access when the browser asks for it.
  • For listening sections, audio is generated through the browser's speech engine.
  • For speaking sections, browser speech recognition is used where supported.
  • Do not refresh the page during a test.
  • Results are calculated locally in your browser.
